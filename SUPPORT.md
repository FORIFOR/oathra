# Support / 質問と導入相談

Choose the smallest public channel that contains enough detail to help:

- **Unexpected judgement**: open the [evidence issue form](https://github.com/FORIFOR/oathra/issues/new?template=evidence.yml) with the redacted speaker order, expected result and actual result.
- **Cannot start or operate**: use the [startup issue form](https://github.com/FORIFOR/oathra/issues/new?template=startup.yml) with the OS, Node.js version, install method and relevant error.
- **Use-case or integration question**: start a [Discussion](https://github.com/FORIFOR/oathra/discussions).
- **Private workflow or business inquiry**: use the [private inquiry form](https://forifor.github.io/oathra/#business). Do not put customer details, phone numbers or call transcripts in a public issue.

Before posting publicly, remove names, phone numbers, credentials, recordings and private transcript text. A short redacted utterance sequence is enough for an evidence report. The simulator and CI are verification tools; they are not production phone reliability or customer adoption evidence.

公開の報告には氏名・電話番号・認証情報・録音・私的な通話内容を含めないでください。実際の業務内容は非公開フォームで相談できます。
